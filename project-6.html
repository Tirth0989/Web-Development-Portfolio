NATURE — 2022

Underworld

LOCATION

New Zealand, Japan, Norway

YEAR

2022

CATEGORY

Nature

FORMAT

Macro / Digital

The forest floor is a city. Most people walk over it without ever looking down.

Underworld is a macro photography series dedicated to ecosystems invisible at walking pace — lichen colonies, mycelium networks, moss forests and insect civilisations that exist beneath our feet. Photographed across New Zealand's ancient podocarp forests, the cedar groves of Yakushima in Japan, and the boreal birch forests of Norway, each image was taken at ground level. The result is a portrait of a world that sustains our own.
