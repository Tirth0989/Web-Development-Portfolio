EDITORIAL — 2023

Matter

LOCATION

Various Studios

YEAR

2023

CATEGORY

Editorial

FORMAT

Medium Format Digital

Objects carry the memory of hands that made them. This series gives them the silence they deserve.

Matter is a collection of editorial still-life commissions produced for design, architecture and lifestyle publications throughout 2023. Each image is constructed as a conversation between surface, light and negative space. The brief was always the same: make the ordinary look inevitable. Ceramics, glass, stone, textile — photographed not as products but as subjects with their own interior logic.
