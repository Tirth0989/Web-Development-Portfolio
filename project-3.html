STREET — 2023

Passage

LOCATION

Tokyo, New York, Lisbon

YEAR

2023

CATEGORY

Street

FORMAT

35mm Film

Cities are full of invisible choreography — the ballet of strangers who will never meet twice.

Passage began in Tokyo's rainy season, following figures through alleys, underpasses, metro corridors and wet-stone market lanes. It expanded across New York and Lisbon — three cities with wildly different rhythms but a shared quality: the profound solitude that exists in the densest crowds. Shot entirely on 35mm film, every frame holds the grain of a specific moment that can never be revisited.
