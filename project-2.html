LANDSCAPE — 2024

Meridian

LOCATION

Patagonia, Iceland, Scottish Highlands

YEAR

2024

CATEGORY

Landscape

FORMAT

Large Format / Digital

Tracing the moment light becomes geography — where the land and sky negotiate the colour of dusk.

Meridian is a long-form landscape study across three of the world's most dramatically lit regions. It began as an attempt to photograph the exact moment of civil twilight — that 30-minute window between sunset and dark — and evolved into a meditation on impermanence and horizon. Every image was captured in a single, unrepeatable light condition. No two frames share the same sky.
