ARCHITECTURE — 2024

Silent Geometries

LOCATION

Berlin, Tokyo, São Paulo

YEAR

2024

CATEGORY

Architecture

FORMAT

35mm / Medium Format

An exploration of the spaces between walls, where light and shadow create a language of their own.

This series investigates the invisible architecture of brutalist spaces — the way light negotiates with concrete, the moments where structure meets void. Shot across three continents over two years, each frame captures the tension between human scale and monumental form. Brutalism is often dismissed as cold, but these photographs argue the opposite: that concrete, when lit correctly, breathes.
