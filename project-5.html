PORTRAIT — 2022

Vigil

LOCATION

Amsterdam, Montreal, Seoul

YEAR

2022

CATEGORY

Portrait

FORMAT

Medium Format Film

Portraits of people not performing for the camera — caught in the private grammar of their own stillness.

Vigil is a long-form portrait project built around a single constraint: every subject was photographed in a moment of genuine quiet. No direction, no posing, no gaze into the lens. The camera arrived after trust was already established — sometimes weeks later. Shot on medium format film across Amsterdam, Montreal and Seoul, each portrait is a study in what presence looks like when no one is performing it.
